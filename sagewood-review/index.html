How we'd approach owner outreach for your two initiatives

Ben — you asked how we'd approach two different markets, where the best opportunity is, and what we'd measure. Short version: we start from what you're already running in Instantly, keep what's working, and only then pilot one clear lane.

Audit first · Instantly baseline One pilot · not parallel rebuild Scoreboard · accepted opps + cost

We would test one defined segment and judge it on accepted owner opportunities and acquisition cost. No pricing in this note.

02 Approach

How we'd work

Protect what's working. Pilot one lane. Expand only with evidence.

First we audit your current Instantly setup with you: niche, offer, copy, list source, volume, replies, how you qualify, meetings booked vs attended, opportunities you actually accepted, and all-in spend. We preserve whichever campaigns are working and recommend only targeted changes.

If you want help on the new Colorado Main Street build, we'd run one pilot there — not a parallel rebuild of national. Colorado-first is a working hypothesis for the new division, not a claim it's easier or better. More niches or geos come later, only after the pilot supports expansion.

Owner-facing conversation

Cold outreach would invite an initial Sagewood conversation about the owner's ownership goals, whether selling is worth exploring, and whether there's brokerage/advisory fit. You confirm the exact deliverable. We won't invent valuations, buyers, or sale readiness in the email. We record sale timing and existing representation as reported by the owner. A qualified call counts as attended only when an owner meeting the agreed criteria actually joins. Estimated business data and owner-reported financials are labeled as such.

Operating process

1 · AUDIT

Lock offer and exclusions after Instantly baseline.

2 · LIST

Build/verify focused owner list; dedupe vs active outreach, relationships, suppressions.

3 · RAMP

Ramp sends (planning capacity up to ~1,000 total emails/day incl. follow-ups — not a commitment; subject to infrastructure, deliverability, audience).

4 · QUALIFY

Verified owner contact where available, written fit filters, explicit interest.

5 · SCORE

You accept/decline; track attended qualified calls and fully loaded cost per accepted opportunity. Zero accepted → report cost and zero (cost/opp not yet calculable).

Signed mandates and fees are outcomes to review in economics discussions, not promises from email volume.

04 Measurement

What we'd measure

Primary

  • Accepted owner opportunities (you accept documented fit + interest)
  • Attended qualified calls
  • Fully loaded cost per accepted opportunity

Secondary

  • Sends
  • Reply mix
  • Booked vs attended

Your baseline

A plain read of a recent cohort — accepted opportunities, attended calls, engagements, fees collected, all-in costs including labor, fee contribution, lag to collection, and acceptable cost per accepted opportunity — so later outsourcing math is grounded in your numbers.
