02 Approach

How we'd work

We'd protect what already works, run one pilot, and expand only if that pilot supports it.

First we'd go through your current Instantly setup with you. We'd look at the niche, the offer, the copy, where the list comes from, how much you send, the replies, how you qualify, meetings booked versus meetings attended, opportunities you accepted, and what you spend all in. We'd leave the campaigns that are working alone, and only suggest a change where something is off.

If you want help on the new Colorado Main Street build, we'd run one pilot there. We would not rebuild the national work at the same time. Starting in Colorado is an idea for the new division. It's not a claim that Colorado is easier or better. More niches or more places come later, and only if the pilot supports that.

What the email would say

The cold email would invite the owner to a first conversation with Sagewood. That conversation is about their goals as an owner, whether selling is worth a look, and whether brokerage or advisory work is a fit. You would confirm the exact offer before we send anything. We would not put a valuation, a buyer, or a claim that they are ready to sell in the email. We'd write down sale timing and any current representation the way the owner states them. A call counts as attended only when an owner who meets the rules you set shows up and joins. If a number is an estimate, or if the owner reported it, we'd mark it that way.

How the work would run

1. Audit

Set the offer, and who is out, after we see how Instantly is performing now.

2. List

Build a focused list of owners and check it. Take off anyone you already email, already know, or have asked us not to contact.

3. Ramp

Increase sends over time. The planning figure is up to about 1,000 total emails a day, including follow-ups. That's a planning number, not a promise. It depends on the sending setup, whether mail is landing, and how many owners fit.

4. Qualify

Use a checked owner contact where we have one, the written fit rules, and a clear yes from the owner.

5. Score

You accept or decline. We track qualified calls the owner joins, and the all-in cost per accepted opportunity. If you accept none, we report the spend and zero accepted opportunities. We do not turn that zero into a cost per opportunity.

A signed engagement and any fee are things we look at when we review the numbers together. Sending more email does not promise either one.

04 Measurement

What we'd measure

Primary

  • Opportunities you accept, where the owner fits the rules and has said they are interested
  • Qualified calls the owner joins
  • All-in cost per accepted opportunity, including labor

Secondary

  • Sends
  • What kinds of replies come back
  • Meetings booked versus meetings attended

Your current numbers

A plain read of a recent stretch of outreach. Opportunities you accepted, calls attended, engagements you signed, fees you collected, and all-in costs including labor. Also what those fees contributed after costs, how long it took to get paid, and what cost per accepted opportunity is acceptable to you. That way any later talk about handing this off uses your numbers.
