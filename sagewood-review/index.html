What we heard

Nationwide sell-side

Your work across the country has two sectors. One is Outdoors and Recreation. The other is Manufacturing, Industrial and Specialty Trades.

Colorado Main Street

You are building a separate brokerage for smaller firms the owner runs. The industry set is wider than the national pair. This is its own initiative.

What you use

You use Instantly. You can pass us other parts of outbound in 2027 if the math supports it. Results matter to you. A fresh list on its own does not.

How we work

We start in the Instantly account you run. You and I go through the live motion from niche and offer into copy and name source and volume and replies and fit. Attended meetings belong in that pass. So do accepted owner opportunities and the all-in spend.

A campaign that produces good conversations stays in place. We change a step that fails. We do not rebuild national sell-side and Colorado Main Street in one pilot.

You pick the pilot. It can be Colorado Main Street or the national sell-side.

A Main Street start is an idea for the new division. It is not a claim that Colorado is easier or better. More niches or places wait until this pilot supports them. How many owner talks we book depends on what your team can take.

The cold email invites the owner to a first Sagewood talk on goals and a possible sale. We check whether brokerage or advice fits. You confirm the offer before we send it.

That note does not state a business value or name a buyer or claim a readiness to sell. We record sale timing and any current representation as stated. We label estimates and owner-reported numbers with their source.

What we do

We stand beside the outbound you built. We read it with you before we change it and we keep the part that works. The new effort is one pilot with boundaries you set for place and industry and size. Lists are owners we have checked. People you email or know or asked us to skip stay off that list.

Signed work and fees are a separate conversation. They are not an owner opportunity you accept. Email volume does not promise a signature or a fee.

The path below is the work itself. It runs from the audit to the score.

1

Audit

We set the offer and who does not fit after we see how Instantly performs. We do not change a live campaign before that review.

2

List

We build a focused owner list and we check it. Anyone you email or know or asked us to skip comes off.

3

Ramp

We raise sends in steps. The planning ceiling is up to 1000 total emails a day, with follow-ups and existing outbound inside that number. Capacity sets the pace. This is not a commitment.

4

Qualify

When we have a checked owner contact we use it, along with the fit rules you write. A clear yes is required before a meeting goes on your calendar.

5

Score

You say yes or no on each owner opportunity. We record attended qualified calls and all-in cost against that count.

What we measure

We do not have a verified baseline for your campaigns. This page sets no target for opportunities or calls or cost.

An owner opportunity you accept has documented fit and interest. Signed work and a fee are separate outcomes. They are not this count.

Accepted owner opportunities

You accept or decline each one. Fit and interest are written down. That is the count we track.

Attended qualified calls

A call counts when an owner who meets your rules joins. A booking that nobody attends does not count.

All-in cost per accepted opportunity

Labor is part of the number. If none are accepted, we report the spend beside zero and we do not invent a unit cost.

If you hand us more outbound, that decision uses a recent account read of opportunities you accept and calls attended and signed work and fees collected.

That read includes all-in cost with labor and the time to collect and the limit per accepted opportunity.

It includes what fees add after cost.
