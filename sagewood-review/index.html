02 Approach

How we work

We keep what works and we run one pilot. We add to it if the pilot supports that.

We go through your Instantly setup with you. We look at these items.

  • Niche
  • Offer
  • Copy
  • List source
  • Send volume
  • Replies
  • How you decide who fits
  • Meetings you book
  • Meetings the owner attends
  • Deals you accept
  • All-in spend

We leave a campaign that works as it is. We change a step that fails.

If you want help on the new Colorado Main Street build, we run one pilot there. We do not rebuild the national work while this pilot runs. We try Colorado for the new division. This is an idea, not a claim that Colorado is easier or better. We add niches or places if the pilot supports that.

What the email says

The cold email invites the owner to a first talk with Sagewood. The talk covers owner goals, and it covers whether a sale is worth a look. It covers whether brokerage or advice is a fit. You confirm the offer before we send the email.

We do not put these in the email.

  • A value for the business
  • A named buyer
  • A claim that the owner is ready to sell

We record sale timing the way the owner states it. We record who represents the owner the way the owner states it. A call counts as attended when an owner who meets your rules joins the call. If a figure is an estimate, we label it an estimate. If the owner gives a figure, we label it as the owner's figure.

How the work runs

1. Audit

We set the offer and who does not fit after we see how Instantly performs.

2. List

We build a focused owner list and we check it. We remove these people.

  • People you email
  • People you know
  • People you told us to skip
3. Ramp

We raise the send count in steps. The plan cap is one thousand emails a day. That count includes the next emails in the thread. This number is a plan, not a promise. The cap depends on three things.

  • The sending setup
  • Whether mail lands
  • How many owners fit
4. Qualify

We use a checked owner contact when we have one, and we use the fit rules you write. We need a clear yes from the owner.

5. Score

You accept a deal or you decline it. We track calls the owner joins and the all-in cost per deal you accept. If you accept no deals, we report the spend and zero deals. We do not turn that zero into a cost per deal.

We review signed work and fees with the numbers. Extra email does not promise signed work or a fee.
