Sagewood and Sentience Growth: Owner outreach

Build a measurable pipeline of owners interested in working with Sagewood.

We show the plan for both markets first. Next we review your live outbound campaigns and recommend fixes. Then we launch Colorado Main Street. Nationwide Outdoors and Manufacturing follow later under the same sending setup.

Accepted owner opportunities

You accept or decline each opportunity against documented fit and owner interest. We track these apart from calls.

Attended qualified calls

The owner fits Sagewood's agreed criteria, wants your brokerage or advisory service, and attends. We map the exact criteria with you before launch.

What we know about you

Nationwide sell-side

Outdoors & Recreation; Manufacturing / Industrial / Specialty Trades. Founder-led. Broad sizes. Nationwide.

Colorado Main Street

Brokerage division you are spinning up. Colorado first. Smaller owner-operated firms. Wider industries.

Both seek owner conversations for Sagewood. They need different fit rules, lists, messages, and reporting. Main Street is geography-first with broader industries. Nationwide is sector-first with broader geography. Separate lists, copy, exclusions, reporting. Nationwide has two industry lanes; Main Street has its own campaign.

This is not a curated list or an appointment-setter package. We connect fit-based sourcing, controlled message tests, interest checks, opportunity handoff, and outcome-led iteration.

What we design before we see your campaign data

We can design this now, before we see your current outbound setup:

  • Separate market and subsegment hypotheses. Provisional fit rules and exclusions (you confirm later).
  • Candidate sourcing, company and site checks, owner verification, enrichment, and suppression.
  • Segment-and-angle test cells. Change one useful variable at a time. Record which company and owner gets which version.
  • First-touch and follow-up purposes. Timing from first touch. Bounded unique-owner allocations and max email budgets in the tables below.
  • Review, scale, and pause rules.

A directory entry is not sale intent. A valid email is not a qualified opportunity.

Where we start

Start with Colorado Main Street. It is a sensible first test for the new division: one geography, a bounded cell, and a clean scoreboard. That is a sequencing choice. It is not a claim Main Street converts better than nationwide work.

We would switch that order if Nationwide Outdoors or Manufacturing shows stronger early signals — for example if those lanes already convert and have spare conversation capacity, or if Main Street is not ready to take owner conversations.

Planning assumption: no prior Main Street campaigns unless your outbound review shows otherwise. We preserve existing outbound wherever it works and recommend changes where the review finds a problem. We launch one market at a time.

How we count

Accepted owner opportunities

Documented fit plus expressed interest. You accept or decline against agreed rules. We track submitted vs accepted. Acceptance stays apart from attendance.

Attended qualified calls

Owner fits Sagewood's ICP, wants brokerage or advisory service, and shows up. We track bookings apart from attendance. An interested reply is not an attended call.

Diagnostics: unique owners, total emails, replies, deliverability. Targets follow campaign review and written fit rules. We state what we test and measure. We do not forecast Sagewood call or deal results from this page.

Roadmap

1

Desk-work design

Separate hypotheses, provisional fit rules, sourcing recipes, angle cells, sequence, and bounded allocations above.

2

Review live campaigns

You already run Instantly. That setup stays in place: we work alongside it, clean up what is clearly off, and de-dupe any new list against those campaigns before the first send. Keep what works. Recommend concrete fixes. Feed findings into Main Street. Whether to outsource more of that work as you head into 2027 is a decision from the results.

3

Sending readiness

Check domains, inboxes, and deliverability. Default fresh domains for a shared setup of up to 1,000 emails per day, including follow-ups across managed outbound (shared, not per lane). Coordinate existing outbound so lists and sends do not overlap. Consider managing your current setup only if it is healthy and already supports that volume and inbox management. Ramp. Pause if bounce or reply quality degrades. Readiness thresholds come from the setup review.

4

Validate Main Street

Company fit before send. Owner checks. Suppress existing contacts, do-not-contact lists, and live campaigns.

5

Ramp Main Street

Under the shared 1,000/day setup alone. Score accepted owner opportunities apart from attended qualified calls.

6

Review, then nationwide later

Scale, pause, or rewrite Main Street from the scoreboard. Then evaluate a national test under the same shared ceiling. Not a simultaneous launch.
