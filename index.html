Qualified meetings, booked onto your calendar

Business owners who need funding, screened against your criteria. Here's how we run it.

✓

Guarantee: 10 qualified meetings in 30 days from launch

If we book fewer than 10 qualified meetings in the first 30 days after launch, you may cancel and receive a refund of the per-meeting fees you paid. Conditions apply:

  • When the 30 days start: at campaign launch, after the inbox warmup. Any delay on your side is added to the end of the 30 days.
  • Approvals: you approve the targeting and email copy within 48 hours of receiving them.
  • Follow-up: you follow up with leads within 24 hours.
  • No-shows and late cancellations: if you miss a booked meeting or cancel it late, the meeting counts toward the 10.
  • Disputes: you have 24 hours after a meeting to dispute whether it was qualified, with a reason. After that, it counts.
  • What's covered: the guarantee covers qualified meetings booked, not deals closed.
  • Targeting lock: Targeting is locked once you approve it. If you need to change your ideal customer, territory, or funding criteria after launch, we can rebuild the list as a paid add-on, and the 30-day guarantee no longer applies to that campaign.
  • Refunds: cover only the per-meeting fees you paid, and only if you've met these conditions and are current on invoices. Refunds never include the $750 setup fee or the website.
  • If you take a refund: campaign work stops, and the domains and inboxes are transferred to you where the provider allows it.
